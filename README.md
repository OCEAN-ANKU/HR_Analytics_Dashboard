## Overview
This project is an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI.  
The dashboard helps analyze employee attrition, workforce distribution, salary trends, job roles, and overall HR performance metrics.

---

## Business Problem
Organizations often struggle to understand employee attrition patterns and workforce behavior.  
This dashboard helps HR teams identify attrition trends, analyze employee demographics, and make data-driven workforce decisions.

---
## Business Problem
Organizations often struggle to identify the key factors behind employee attrition and workforce performance.

This dashboard helps HR teams:
- Monitor employee attrition
- Analyze department-wise workforce distribution
- Track salary and experience trends
- Support HR decision-making using data insights

## Key Insights
- Research & Development department has the highest employee count.
- Sales department shows comparatively higher attrition.
- Employees with lower experience are more likely to leave.
- Certain job roles contribute significantly to attrition.
- Salary and experience influence employee retention.
- Dashboard enables interactive HR analysis using filters and slicers.
